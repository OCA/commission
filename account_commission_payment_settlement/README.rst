This module settles payment based commissions according to the payments of the
invoices:

* An invoice is settled only when its last payment is before the date limit of
  the settlement wizard, the same limit applied to the invoice date. Settling a
  past period later doesn't include the invoices paid after that period.
* A payment counts on its due date when it is later than its date, as a payment
  order confirmed before its collection date. Refunds count on the
  reconciliation date.
* When a payment of an invoice with settled commissions is undone (a payment
  return, removing the payment from the invoice, resetting the payment or the
  invoice to draft), the next settlement includes a negative line for the
  settled amount, dated on the day the payment was undone. When the invoice is
  paid again, a later settlement includes the commission again.

Configuration
=============

#. Go to *Commissions > Configuration > Commission types*.
#. Set *Invoice Status* to *Payment Based* on the commission types to settle
   according to the payments.

Commission types based on the invoice are not affected by this module.

Usage
=====

#. Go to *Commissions > Settlements > Settle Commissions*.
#. Set the *Up to* date. The limit isn't included: to settle July, set the 1st
   of August. For agents with a settlement period, the limit is the start of the
   period containing that date.
#. Payment based commissions of invoices paid before the limit are settled.
   Invoices with a payment on or after the limit wait for a later settlement.
#. Settlements of invoices whose payment was undone before the limit get a
   negative line, and a positive one when the invoice is paid again before a
   later limit.

If a settlement is cancelled, its amounts are pending again and the next
settlement includes them.
